Between Us Support

Hi. Thanks for using Between Us.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and enter both of your names.
  2. Pick a questionnaire. One person answers, then hands the phone to the other.
  3. When you have both finished, compare your answers side by side.
  4. Use Lightning Round for quick back-and-forth questions, or Guided Conversation when you want to talk something through and each commit to one small thing.

FAQ

Does the other person see my answers before they finish?

The compare screen stays hidden until you have both submitted. The app does not use a passcode, so treat the unlocked phone as shared. If you need to change an answer after comparing, tap Update an answer.

Where is our data stored?

Only on this iPhone. There is no account and no sync to another phone. Deleting the app deletes your answers and agreements.

Can we use it on two phones?

Not yet. Between Us is built for one shared phone.

How do Lightning Round and Guided Conversation work?

Lightning Round shows one question. You both answer it out loud, then pass the phone for the next one. Guided Conversation walks you from picking a topic, to each person being heard, to brainstorming, to saving one small commitment each.
