Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Overview

Between Us ("we," "our," or "the app") is a private couples app for two spouses — shared on one iPhone, or paired across each spouse's own iPhone. This policy explains what the app stores and how.

Short version

We don't have a server. We don't collect, transmit, or sell any of your data, and we cannot read it. Your data lives on your phone — and, only if you choose to pair two phones, in your own personal iCloud accounts, where Apple's privacy protections apply and we have no access.

What the App Stores

The app saves the following on the device, in a local file:

  • The two names you enter
  • Questionnaire answers
  • Notes you write to each other, including private ones you have not shared yet
  • Agreements from guided conversations
  • Affirmations you write yourself

Uninstalling the app deletes this data from the phone. Anyone who can unlock the phone can open the app and see what is saved there. The app does not lock answers behind a passcode.

Optional iCloud Sync Between Two Phones

If you choose to pair two phones, the same data syncs between them through Apple iCloud (CloudKit), using a private shared area that only the two invited iCloud accounts can access. In that case:

  • Your data is stored in your own iCloud, under Apple's privacy policy and security. It never passes through any server of ours — we don't have one, and we cannot see, read, or recover your data.
  • Only the two of you are in the share. Notes marked "kept back" sync too (so they aren't lost if you replace your phone), but the app shows them only to the spouse who wrote them until that person chooses to share them.
  • You can stop syncing at any time in the app's settings. To remove synced data from iCloud entirely, the spouse who created the pairing can stop sharing, and data can be managed from iCloud settings.

If you never pair two phones, nothing leaves the phone at all.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect personal information and send it anywhere.
  • We do not collect analytics or usage data.
  • We do not use tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • We do not have user accounts or sign-in.
  • We do not use cookies.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.
  • We do not request access to your location, microphone, contacts, photos, or camera.

Children's Privacy

Between Us is for adults in a relationship. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
